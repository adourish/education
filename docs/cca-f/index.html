Reading settings

Nothing here changes your answers. Everything is remembered in this browser, so each machine can be set up the way it suits you there.

Theme The last three come from a code editor and a terminal.
Typeface No typeface is proven best for everyone. Try each and keep the one you read fastest.
Text size
Line spacing More air between lines makes it easier to keep your place.
Letter and word spacing Wider gaps between letters and words is the change with the strongest evidence behind it.
Line length Short lines are easier to follow back to the start.
Choosing an answer One tap answers straight away. Tap then confirm lets you rest on an answer and read it before committing, which is what makes the reading ruler usable on a touch screen, where there is no pointer to hover with.
Reading ruler Marks the line you are pointing at with a bar down its left, the way a ruler under a line of print stops the eye dropping to the wrong one. Nothing is tinted, because a colour behind words costs contrast. It marks one line at a time, so turn bullet points on or there are no separate lines to mark. On a phone, set "Choosing an answer" to tap then confirm, or a tap answers before you can read it.
Spacing out what you got wrong A question you put right comes back after a day, then three, then a week, then a fortnight, then a month. Get it wrong and the wait starts over. Answering something again an hour later teaches far less than answering it again next week.
Bold Heavy letters sit closer together, which can make them harder to tell apart.
What to call this device Shown against any half-finished set that was begun somewhere else, so a list of them tells you which is the one from your phone. Guessed from the browser unless you say otherwise. It stays in this browser and in files you export yourself, and is not sent anywhere.

This browser will not save your progress

The board keeps your answers in this browser's local storage, and this browser is refusing it. That usually means one of:

  • a private or incognito window
  • site data turned off, or blocked for this site
  • storage already full

Everything still works — drill as normal. But when you close this tab, your answers go with it. Export the file before you finish and import it next time.
