Memorization Notes · SAA-C03

How to use this
Every page is built for recall, not for first-time learning. Cover the right-hand column of any table, say the answer out loud, then uncover and check.

Cue → Answer tables are facts and definitions. Numbers tables are pure rote — do those first. Mnemonics are for lists you must produce from nothing. Triggers map an exam scenario to the service that answers it, and are worth the most marks per minute of study.

Highlighted callouts are traps — the places where a plausible-looking answer is wrong.

Start with Numbers and Limits, finish with Decision Triggers, and drill Rapid Fire daily in the last two weeks.
